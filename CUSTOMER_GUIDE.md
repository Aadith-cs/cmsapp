# Canteen Management System - Customer Guide

Welcome to the Canteen Management System! This guide will help you browse the menu, place orders, and track them.

## Getting Started
1.  **Access the App**: Open the application URL in your browser.
2.  **No Login Required**: Valid for browsing and ordering as a guest (or student).

## Placing an Order
### 1. Browse Menu
- Navigate to the **Home** page.
- You will see a list of available menu items categorized by type (e.g., Snacks, Meals).
- Use the category filters at the top to find what you want quickly.

### 2. Add to Cart
- Click the **"Add to Cart"** button on any item.
- You can add multiple items.
- The **Cart** icon in the navigation bar will show the number of items added.

### 3. Review Cart
- Click on the **Cart** link in the navigation bar.
- Review your items. You can:
    - Increase or decrease quantity.
    - Remove items.
- The total price is displayed at the bottom.

### 4. Checkout
- Click **"Proceed to Checkout"**.
- Review your order summary.
- Enter your mock payment details (for this demo version).
- Click **"Place Order"**.

### 5. Order Confirmation
- Upon success, you will see an **Order Success** page.
- A **QR Code** and **Order ID** will be generated.
- **Save this QR Code!** You will need to show it to the canteen staff to collect your food.

## Tracking Your Order
- Currently, you can track the status by asking the admin or waiting for your number to be called/displayed on the public screen (if enabled).
